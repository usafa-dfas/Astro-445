    Model

    Elementary rotations
    ROT3(φ) = [cos φ, sin φ, 0; −sin φ, cos φ, 0; 0, 0, 1], and likewise for ROT1, ROT2 (Lesson 3, App. A)
    Euler angles, sequence i-j-k
    CB/A = ROTk(φ) ROTj(θ) ROTi(ψ)
    Axis and angle
    CB/A = I cos φB/A + eeT(1 − cos φB/A) − [e×] sin φB/A
    e = [c23 − c32, c31 − c13, c12 − c21]T / 2 sin φB/A
    Quaternion (Euler parameters)
    ε = e sin(φB/A/2),   η = cos(φB/A/2)
    CB/A = (η² − εTε)I + 2εεT − 2η[ε×]
    η = ½(1 + tr C)½,   ε = [c23 − c32, c31 − c13, c12 − c21]T / 4η

    The app stores the attitude as a unit quaternion and computes every other panel from it. Quaternion from DCM uses Shepperd's method, which divides by the largest of η, ε1, ε2, ε3. The axis is also found as the null vector of C − I, the eigenvector for λ = 1. Euler angles come from the DCM with θ taken in 0° to 180° for symmetric sequences and −90° to 90° for asymmetric ones.
