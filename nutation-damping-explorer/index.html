    New to this? Key terms
    Nutation (wobble)
    The spin axis circling around the angular momentum h̄ instead of lining up with it. The nutation angle θ measures how far apart they are.
    Nutation damper
    A device that turns wobble into heat, so the wobble dies away. Real ones use friction, fluid sloshing in a ring, or small rotors.
    −bω model
    The lesson's simple model of a damper: a torque from outside that pushes back against any sideways turning. Easy to analyze, but it also drains h̄, which no real on-board damper can.
    Rotor damper
    Appendix B's model: small wheels inside the stage, dragged along by friction. Everything happens inside, so h̄ stays fixed and only energy is lost.
    Angular momentum h̄
    The stage's "amount of spin". Only an outside torque, such as the engine, can change it. Internal parts can only pass it back and forth.
    Oblate and prolate
    Oblate: squat, spinning about the axis with the largest inertia (Ia > It). Prolate: tall, spinning about the axis with the smallest (Ia < It).
    Energy sink
    With h̄ fixed, losing energy always pushes a spin toward the axis with the largest inertia. Oblate: toward θ = 0, the wobble dies. Prolate: toward θ = 90°, a flat spin.
    Flat spin
    Tumbling end over end about a sideways axis. It is where a prolate stage with a real damper ends up, as the Explorer 1 satellite did in 1958.
