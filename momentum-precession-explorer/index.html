    New to this? Key terms
    Momentum bias
    Keeping a wheel spinning inside a platform that itself does not spin. The wheel's angular momentum makes the platform hard to turn, like a gyroscope, while its cameras and antennas stay steadily pointed.
    Angular momentum h̄
    The spin "stored" in the vehicle, mostly in the wheel. A torque changes it at a rate equal to the torque, so it moves in the direction the torque points: angular momentum follows torque.
    Precession
    The steady creep of the wheel axis toward the torque, at the rate c/hs (torque divided by wheel momentum). It lasts as long as the torque does.
    Nutation (wobble)
    The small circling of the wheel axis on top of the creep, of size cIt/hs² and frequency hs/It.
    Cycloid
    The curve traced by a point on the rim of a rolling wheel. Creep plus wobble makes the wheel axis trace one.
    Requirement
    The pointing limits a mission sets, here how far the axis may drift during the burn and how much it may wobble.
    Momentum dumping
    Removing the momentum a torque has added, with thrusters or magnetic torquers. The wheel only slows the drift; the accumulated momentum cT must eventually be removed by active control.
    Internal and external damping
    Internal damping (sloshing fuel, flexing panels, a damper) turns wobble into heat but cannot change the total angular momentum. External damping is a torque from outside, such as a controller firing thrusters, and it does change it.
