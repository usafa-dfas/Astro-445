    New to this? Key terms
    State feedback
    The controller measures every state, here the angle and the rate, and sets the torque to a fixed combination of them, u = −Kx.
    Cost J
    A score for the whole response: the integral of the weighted squared error plus the weighted squared torque. LQR finds the K with the smallest score.
    Q and R
    How much you care about each state error and about effort. Raise Q to tighten a state; raise R to calm the actuator. Only their ratio matters.
    Bryson's rule
    Weight each variable by one over the square of the largest value you will accept, so every term is dimensionless and equals 1 at its limit.
    Riccati equation
    AᵀP + PA − PBR⁻¹BᵀP + Q = 0. Its solution P gives the gain K = R⁻¹BᵀP and the minimum cost xᵀPx.
    Poles
    The eigenvalues of A − BK: how fast and how oscillatory the closed loop is. Settling takes about 4 divided by the slowest decay rate.

      The Riccati equation is solved numerically, by the matrix sign function of the Hamiltonian [A, −BR−1BT; −Q, −AT], the same answer MATLAB's lqr gives. Each response is integrated with fixed-step RK4, along with the running cost. Weights given in degrees are converted to radians before solving, so the gains shown are always in N·m/rad and N·m·s/rad. Settling time is the notes' 4 divided by the slowest pole's decay rate. Ir = 18, Iy = 8 kg·m² and a 600 km orbit set kgg.
