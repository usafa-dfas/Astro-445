Attitude from straight down

Yaw, then pitch, then roll (a 3-2-1 sequence) from the local-level frame: o1 along track, o3 straight down.

Orbit and field

The spacecraft points one face straight down (nadir pointing), so its body axes are the local-level axes: b1 along track, b2 across, b3 down.

Camera

A 20° field of view on 1024 pixels (70″ each). Stars are spread over about three pixels by a slight defocus, which helps find their centers precisely.

The Star Tracker Explorer shows how the attitude is computed from the stars (Wahba's problem).

Gyroscope

The proof mass is driven back and forth 10 µm at 10 kHz, so its top speed is 0.63 m/s.
