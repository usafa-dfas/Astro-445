Polhode Explorer

Angular velocity space in the principal-axis frame. With no external torque, T and H are constant, so the tip of ω must lie on both the energy ellipsoid and the momentum ellipsoid. It travels along their intersection: the polhode.

Energy dissipation

Leave at 0 for a rigid body. A positive ν (s⁻¹) adds an internal energy sink: T falls while H stays fixed, so the polhode spirals toward the major axis (the major-axis rule, as in Explorer 1).
