Gyrostat Explorer

A rigid platform with one wheel along a principal axis, run three times side by side with different wheel momentum hs. Stored wheel momentum moves the spin out of the unstable band, a damper decides which of two equally good rigid designs really settles, and with no spin at all the wheel alone holds the platform still.

      All three runs integrate the full nonlinear equations and the attitude quaternion with fixed-step RK4. The motor either holds the wheel's speed relative to the platform, or applies a constant torque gs until the wheel reaches its hs, splitting the step at that instant. The damper is Lesson 25's Appendix B rotor pair, mounted on the two transverse axes; I1, I2, I3 include the wheel and the rotors as if locked. With the damper on, the "with damper" rows come from the four-state linear model about the steady spin. The inertial frame is the starting body frame.
