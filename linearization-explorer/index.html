    New to this? Key terms
    Linearize
    Replace the equations near one operating point by their tangent: a matrix that turns a small change in the state into a small change in its rate.
    Operating point, equilibrium
    The state the model is built around. An equilibrium is one where nothing changes, so small departures from it are all that the linear model has to describe.
    Jacobian
    The matrix of partial derivatives ∂fi/∂xj, here evaluated at the operating point. It is A (for the state) and B (for the input).
    Eigenvalue, mode
    Each eigenvalue of A is a natural motion. An imaginary pair ±jω is an oscillation at ω; a positive real part grows; zero means drift.
    Nutation
    The fast coning of a body carrying stored momentum, here from the pitch-wheel bias, at about |h₂|/√(IrIy).
