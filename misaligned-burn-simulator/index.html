Misaligned Burn Simulator

An axisymmetric upper stage spins up, then fires its main engine with a misalignment torque fixed in the body. Spin couples the transverse rates at the relative spin rate Ω, so a torque that would tumble the vehicle produces only a bounded wobble, and p̂3 traces an epicycloid about its starting direction.

    Euler's equations, axisymmetric
    Itω1 = (It − Ia) ω2ω3 + g1
    Itω2 = (Ia − It) ω1ω3 + g2
    Iaω3 = g3
    Step torque c about p̂1
    ω1 = (c/ItΩ) sin Ωt,   ω2 = −(c/ItΩ)(1 − cos Ωt)
    Step torque c about p̂2
    ω1 = (c/ItΩ)(1 − cos Ωt),   ω2 = (c/ItΩ) sin Ωt
    Impulse H δ(t) about p̂2
    ω1 = (H/It) sin Ωt,   ω2 = (H/It) cos Ωt
    Epicycloid, torque about p̂1
    θ1 = −Ap(1 − cos ωpt) + An(1 − cos ωnt)
    θ2 = Ap sin ωpt − An sin ωnt

    The simulation integrates the full nonlinear Euler equations and a quaternion with fixed-step RK4, splitting the step exactly at spin-up end, ignition and cutoff. An impulse is applied as an instant jump in the transverse rate. The epicycloid is drawn for a torque about p̂1 and rotated to match the torque axis and the vehicle's spin angle at ignition. A torque about p̂2 gives the same curve turned 90°.
